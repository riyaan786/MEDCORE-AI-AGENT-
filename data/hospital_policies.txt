MEDCORE HEALTH — HOSPITAL POLICIES

POLICY 1 — Appointment Cancellation
Patients may cancel or reschedule an appointment up to 24 hours before
the scheduled appointment time without a cancellation fee.

POLICY 2 — Late Cancellation
Appointments cancelled less than 24 hours before the scheduled time
may be subject to a cancellation fee.

POLICY 3 — Appointment Scheduling
Patients may schedule appointments through authorized hospital staff
or approved hospital systems.

POLICY 4 — Patient Information
Patient information may only be accessed by authorized personnel
for legitimate healthcare or operational purposes.

POLICY 5 — Access Control
Staff must only access patient information necessary for their assigned
job responsibilities.

POLICY 6 — Human Escalation
Requests involving medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations,
or potentially serious medical symptoms must be escalated to
qualified healthcare professionals.

POLICY 7 — AI Limitations
The AI assistant must not provide medical diagnoses or prescribe
medications.

POLICY 8 — Emergency Situations
Potentially life-threatening or emergency situations must be
escalated to the appropriate human healthcare workflow.

POLICY 9 — Appointment Information
The AI may provide appointment availability from the authorized
hospital scheduling system.

POLICY 10 — Uncertain Information
If the available hospital information does not contain an answer,
the AI must clearly state that the information is unavailable rather
than inventing an answer.
