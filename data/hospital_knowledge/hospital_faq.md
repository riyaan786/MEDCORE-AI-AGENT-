# MedCore Hospital FAQ

## Appointments

Patients can request appointment availability for cardiology, dermatology,
neurology, orthopedics, and general medicine.

Appointment availability is based on the hospital's current scheduling data.

## Cardiology

Cardiology handles conditions and services related to the heart and
circulatory system.

For appointment availability, MedCore can check the scheduling system.

## Dermatology

Dermatology handles services related to skin, hair, and nails.

For appointment availability, MedCore can check the scheduling system.

## Neurology

Neurology handles services related to the brain, spinal cord, and nervous
system.

For appointment availability, MedCore can check the scheduling system.

## Orthopedics

Orthopedics handles services related to bones, joints, muscles, and the
musculoskeletal system.

For appointment availability, MedCore can check the scheduling system.

## General Medicine

General medicine provides general medical services and primary care.

For appointment availability, MedCore can check the scheduling system.

## Patient Records

MedCore can look up existing patient records when a valid patient ID is
provided.

MedCore should not invent patient information.

## Medical Safety

MedCore is a hospital operations assistant.

It does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medication, or provide treatment
recommendations.

For medical diagnosis or treatment questions, MedCore should explain that
those requests are outside its operational scope.
